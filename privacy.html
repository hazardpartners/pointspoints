Trust and transparency

Privacy policy.

Our intent is to build useful rewards tools without unnecessary access to your financial life.

Draft—must be finalized before launch. This page outlines intended design principles, not a verified description of a deployed app. Review against actual app behavior, analytics, notifications, third-party services, and applicable law before release.

Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. Overview

PointsPoints is a planned rewards recommendation and planning service. We aim to minimize collection of personal information and to make the core experience available without a bank or loyalty account connection.

2. Information you choose to provide

The app is designed to let you select your cards, configure preferences, and enter rewards goals. These settings are intended to be stored locally where feasible. If you contact us by email, we may receive your message and email address to respond.

3. Device permissions

With your permission, location may support nearby rewards insights; notification permissions may support timely reminders. You should be able to manage these permissions in your device settings. Exact processing and retention practices must be specified once implemented.

4. External services and affiliate links

Some links may direct you to third-party merchant or credit card sites. They may use referral or affiliate tracking subject to those sites' privacy policies. PointsPoints may earn compensation for qualifying actions. We will identify relevant commercial relationships as required.

5. Analytics, retention, and sharing

Before public release, this section must be updated to identify any analytics, crash reporting, push-notification providers, hosting logs, data retention periods, vendors, and personal data sharing practices actually used.

6. Your choices

The intended design minimizes stored personal data. Once the app is complete, this section should describe available access, deletion, permission, and opt-out mechanisms accurately.

7. Children's privacy

PointsPoints is not intended for children under 13. Final age-related language should be reviewed for the markets where the app operates.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service develops. For privacy questions, see our support page.
