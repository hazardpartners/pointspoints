Everything you need to know about the upcoming PointsPoints experience.

Is PointsPoints available yet?

PointsPoints is currently in development. This site introduces the product direction; it does not represent a live App Store listing.

Will I need to connect my bank accounts?

The planned core experience does not require a bank login, loyalty login, or credential sharing. You choose the cards you have.

Does PointsPoints track transactions?

No manual purchase-by-purchase ledger is planned for the core experience. Some issuer-specific spend caps or targeted offers may not be knowable without transactional data.

Is PointsPoints free?

The goal is to make the core features free. Optional affiliate links may help support the product in the future.

How accurate are card recommendations?

Recommendations depend on published card benefits, relevant categories, your settings, and applicable limitations. Always confirm current terms with the card issuer.

Does PointsPoints sell my data?

The product is designed with a local-first philosophy. Refer to the privacy page for the intended approach; the final operational policy should be reviewed before launch.

Is PointsPoints affiliated with credit card companies?

No. PointsPoints is an independent project, and names of issuers or loyalty programs belong to their respective owners.
